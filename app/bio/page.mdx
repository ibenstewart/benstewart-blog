# Bio

<PersonSchema />

I run engineering teams that are working out AI in real time, and I write about it here while it's still messy.

At home I have a wife, a daughter and a dog that I met in that exact order. Engineer turned leader. I build things and help teams build better.

Currently at Skyscanner, working on how technology, product, and people come together to make travel simpler.

## What I write about

Mostly what happens when you run engineering teams through AI. Which tasks I hand over and which I keep, what it cost when I got that wrong, and the old processes teams keep running underneath the new tools. The view behind most of it is that AI adoption is a leadership problem before it's a tooling one. The older posts are about leadership from before AI turned up, and plenty of it still applies.

## Short bio

Ben Stewart is VP of Engineering at Skyscanner, based in Glasgow. He writes field reports at benstewart.ai about leading engineering teams through AI: what's working, what's breaking and what the data says, in plain English. He started his career writing SQL in 2006.
